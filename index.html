I'm an undergraduate studying Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at UC Berkeley, with a minor in Physics. I'm interested in building hardware that helps machines sense and interact with the physical world.

I work on ultrasonic tactile sensing at Carnegie Mellon's LOCI Lab with Dr. Andrew Spielberg, and wearable hand tracking for stroke rehabilitation at Berkeley's Sensorimotor Neural Engineering Lab with Dr. Preeya Khanna.

Research

LOCI Lab, Carnegie Mellon University · Robotics Intern

May 2026 – Present

Designing a camera-free tactile-sensing platform for robotic manipulation, with mixed-signal PCBs and C++ firmware for synchronized ultrasonic excitation and waveform acquisition.

Wearable sensing for stroke rehabilitation

Sensorimotor Neural Engineering Lab, UC Berkeley · Undergraduate Researcher

Feb 2026 – Present

Developing a low-cost wireless wearable for out-of-clinic hand tracking using flexible strain sensors, IMUs, and custom analog circuitry. I'm experimenting with different material formulations to develop strain sensors suited to tracking hand movement. I also analyze hand-motion data to identify the sensor placements that best capture dominant movement patterns.

May 2023 – Jun 2025

Projects & involvement

eFeel, biosignal acquisition & ultrasonic haptics

NeuroTech @ Berkeley

Aug 2025 – Present

I lead eFeel, a team project developing a multimodal tactile sensing glove to interface with a custom robotic limb control system. I work on sensing development and mechanical hardware integration, combining optical sensing for material classification, time-of-flight sensing for proximity, and pressure sensing for force and shear.

Designed and validated a multi-stage EMG acquisition system with analog filtering and ADC protection. I'm also developing an amplification shield for a 64-channel ultrasonic phased array for haptic and levitation applications.
